## Sprint du kien

### Sprint 1: Nen tang va xac thuc

- Clone source goc.
- Chuyen database sang Supabase PostgreSQL.
- Sua build backend/frontend.
- Cau hinh JWT va admin mac dinh.

### Sprint 2: Khoa hoc va giao dien

- Viet hoa giao dien chinh.
- Cai thien trang chu va danh sach khoa hoc.
- Them seed khoa hoc lap trinh mau.
- Quan ly khoa hoc trong admin.

### Sprint 3: Hoc tap va danh gia

- Dang ky khoa hoc.
- Luu tien do hoc.
- Quan ly cau hoi quiz.
- Lam quiz va xem ket qua.

### Sprint 4: Hoan thien bao cao

- Chung chi.
- Dashboard.
- Testcase report.
- SRS/SDD.
- Slide thuyet trinh va demo.
